REFUSED   by the agent wrapper before signing: receiver_not_allowed. Nothing was sent.

4. Worse: the attacker steals the agent key and signs transactions directly
REJECTED  by the NEAR protocol   send 0.05 NEAR straight to the attacker
          this action needs a full-access key (AccessKeyRequiresFullAccessActionError)
REJECTED  by the NEAR protocol   call withdraw (an owner method)
          the key is not allowed to call this method (AccessKeyMethodNameMismatchActionError)
REJECTED  by the NEAR protocol   add a full-access key of their own
          this action needs a full-access key (AccessKeyRequiresFullAccessActionError)
REJECTED  by the NEAR protocol   replace the contract code
          this action needs a full-access key (AccessKeyRequiresFullAccessActionError)
The only thing left is the pay method, so the attacker calls it without the wrapper:
DENIED    by the contract   0.1 NEAR to attacker: receiver_not_allowed   strike 1 of 3
          tx https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/4yS78nAL19428M5YaYG77RDai5afQ7YWxqbS3ubDn5YB
DENIED    by the contract   0.05 NEAR to attacker: receiver_not_allowed   strike 2 of 3
          tx https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/HQakxHSUFwefQScKBKsMdskGSF7ECVzVrmfLvQJaVeC4
DENIED    by the contract   0.01 NEAR to attacker: receiver_not_allowed   strike 3 of 3
          tx https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/exwTjFJjWxrvb46j7R3qrftCMq2hWPsVr2iMBZkZhpr
TRIPPED   three strikes: the contract froze the agent and deleted its key from the account
REJECTED  by the NEAR protocol   one more try with the stolen key
          the key no longer exists on the account (AccessKeyNotFoundActionError)
